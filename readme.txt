Moonlit Cove

Kyren Hart, Abi Muthuraman, Connor Dunn, Walter Leiva

Moonlit Cove is a game where users play as a little whale named Tsuki, who is trapped in a looping nightmare. The objective is to restore the moon shrines glow to break the loop. The player will explore the area to find certain items and characters in order to get to the shrine and restore it. Each loop has a time limit, and once up, the player will reset to the beginning. The player must restore the shrine in 3 loops or else they fail.

Press 1 for Moon Glow

Press 2 for Moon Pearl

Press 3 for Moon Key

Press 0 to reset collectables
