Moonlit Cove

Kyren Hart, Abi Muthuraman, Connor Dunn, Walter Leiva

Moonlit Cove is a game where users play as a little whale named Tsuki, who is trapped in a looping nightmare. The objective is to restore the moon shrine's glow to break the loop. The player will explore the area to find certain items and characters in order to get to the shrine and restore it. Each loop has a time limit, and once it ends, the player will reset to the beginning. The player must restore the shrine in 3 loops or else they fail.

Cheat Codes
    •    Press 1 for Moon Glow
    •    Press 2 for Moon Pearl
    •    Press 3 for Moon Key
    •    Press 0 to reset collectables
    •    Press L to lose two lives
    •    Press 5 for 5 seconds on the clock


Game Instructions:
Play as Tsuki, a baby orca born from the moon’s reflection. The reflection has disappeared, and the night keeps repeating.
Use W, A, S, D, or the arrow keys to move.
Use space to collect items. 
Explore the cove, discover hidden paths, and learn the rhythm of the ocean. The world resets at midnight, but the discoveries help you progress. 

Game Walkthrough:
    1    Begin at Moonlit Cove → Tsuki starts in Moonlit Cove (main area). Whenever midnight comes, Tsuki automatically returns here, and all collected items are reset. 
    2    Tide at Coral Garden --> Tsuki travels to Coral Garden and interacts with Tide (turtle), who explains that something is wrong with the Moon and that the night keeps repeating itself.
    3    Moon Glow → Tsuki explores Moon Jellyfish Path, which unlocks at a certain time (when the countdown reaches 100 seconds) during the loop. Tsuki follows Lumi to find and collect the Moon Glow. 
    4    Moon Key → Tsuki travels to the Sunken Ship, which becomes accessible at a certain time during the loop. Tsuki explores the area to find the Moon Key, which is needed to unlock the Moon Shrine. 
    5    Moon Pearl → Once Tsuki has collected Moon Glow, Tsuki can enter the Kelp Forest. Here, Tsuki explores the area to find the Moon Pearl. 
    6    Moon Shrine → Tsuki can unlock the Moon Shrine with the Moon Key. To complete the game, Tsuki needs to collect all three of these items: Moon Glow, Moon Key, and Moon Pearl. 
    7    Restore the Moon's Reflection and Moonlit Cove → Once all three collectibles have been collected, Tsuki returns them to the Moon Shrine to restore the missing reflection. The cycle breaks, and Moonlit Cove is saved. 
Note: Tsuki returns to the Cove at midnight, and all collectibles are reset. Make sure to plan the exploration to gather all the items before the loop ends, as Tsuki will lose a life (only 3).
